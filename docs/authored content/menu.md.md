### B. Items

Ingredient 0 is always the base. An item takes at most 6 ingredients after it.

**Short** is the name the kitchen's NOW BUILDING readout shows. The full name is too wide
there, because it shares the space with the item's progress pips. The limit depends on
the step count: `12 x chars + 9 x steps <= 127`, which allows 8 characters at 3 steps,
7 at 4, and 5 at 7. The build will enforce it.

## IV. Notes for every era

- Where a real Taco Shop item lists an ingredient with no bin (Monterey Jack, olives,
  green onions, rice, the whole-wheat tortilla), it is dropped from the recipe and noted
  on the item.

- Items left out because ingredient 0 is always the base: those whose base comes
  mid-build (Loco Taco, the real Taco Supreme), and those needing a second tortilla
  (3 Amigos, Borracho, Mexican Pizza) or a bun (Taco Burger).

- The left base station stacks its three bases top to bottom: Small Tortilla, Taco Shell,
  Large Tortilla. The cursor visits each as its own stop.

- Price is flavour only. What a ticket pays comes from difficulty.md: the tip per item,
  the clean bonus and the combo.

- On screen, each bin has a name plate of at most 7 characters, and each item a ticket
  line of at most 12. **Short** follows the rule in Era 1's Items. build.mjs enforces all
  three and fails the build on any that do not fit.

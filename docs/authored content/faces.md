# faces.md

The customers' faces: who can walk in, in each era, and the state of each baked face. A customer is drawn from their era's cast, and the same face speaks for the life of their ticket. The game reads `content/casts.json` and `content/faces/`: those are the master copy and this page mirrors them, so edit the casts and set the eyes in Service Mode (Faces) or in the JSON. A face blinks only once its eyes are set.

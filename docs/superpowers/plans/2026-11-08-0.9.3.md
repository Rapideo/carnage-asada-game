# 0.9.3 Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Ship the rest of the 0.9.x list:
- the small tuning;
- one face per address;
- weather per level, and windy weather;
- winter tracks in the kitchen;
- the simple level card;
- eyes on the 113 new faces;
- Pixel 10 ghost controls;
- version 0.9.3.

**Architecture:** Each item is a self-contained change to the existing modules. New level data (`weather`, `windy`) and new difficulty dials (wind) go through the one validator and Service Mode like every other content. Wind lives in one small new module (`src/55_wind.js`) that the player, the bag and the HUD read. The touch overlay stays in `src/25_pad.js`, drawn in screen space beside the canvas.

**Tech Stack:** Plain browser JS concatenated by `build.mjs` (no imports in `src/`), Node ESM tools, zero dependencies. Tests are in `test/headless.mjs` and `test/admin.mjs`; frames render with `tools/render/frame.mjs`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-08-0.9.3-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- The demo and attract mode must keep working on every level, windy ones included.
- `taco-shop.html` contains no Service Mode code (`— artifact —`).
- The guards stay green:
  - `City.keep` (no porch buried in geometry);
  - `unwedge` (no permanent wedge);
  - the `— hud layout —` width checks;
  - `— collision —`.
- Each task ends with `node build.mjs`, `node test/headless.mjs` and `node test/admin.mjs` all PASS.
- Commits go on branch `v0.9.3` (created from `master` at 9e5d26f), with this trailer:
  `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`

### Task 4: Windy weather

**Files:**
- **Create:** `src/55_wind.js`.
- **Modify:**
  - `src/50_entities.js` (the `Bag` flight drift; the player gust impulse through the normal movement path);
  - `src/70_hud.js` (the windsock);
  - `src/80_game.js` (spawning and updating tumbleweeds, the gust telegraph, the GUST sound via `Audio5`);
  - `src/20_audio.js` (a `gust` sfx);
  - `content/difficulty.json` and the validator (`wind` per difficulty: `strength` 0-1, `gustEvery` [min, max] seconds, `tumbleweeds` count);
  - the Difficulty panel rows, and the `difficulty.md` mirror.
- **Tests:** `test/headless.mjs` (a new `— wind —` section), `test/admin.mjs`.

**Interfaces:**
- **Produces:** `const Wind = { on, dir, strength, gustT, gust (0..1 now), telegraph (0..1), reset(level, diff), update(dt), push(): {x, y} }`.
  - `dir` is an angle chosen per play.
  - `strength` comes from `diff.wind.strength`.
- **The bag** adds `Wind.push()` scaled acceleration while airborne.
- **The player** gets a sideways impulse during a gust, applied before collision so `carBlocked` and `unwedge` still govern.
- **Tumbleweeds:** `class Tumbleweed { update(dt, G), draw(ctx, cam) }`, entries in the y-sorted sprite list. They roll with the wind along streets, and are recycled around the player like traffic. Hitting one bursts it (`Fx`) and gives `p.wobble = 0.4`. There is no fine and nothing solid.
- **Values (Claude's call):**

  | | strength | gustEvery | tumbleweeds |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | easy | 0.35 | [12, 16] | 3 |
  | medium | 0.55 | [10, 14] | 4 |
  | hard | 0.75 | [8, 12] | 5 |
  | supreme | 0.9 | [8, 11] | 6 |

- [ ] **Step 1:** write failing tests:
  - on a windy level, a bag thrown at a fixed aim lands measurably downwind of where it lands with no wind;
  - a gust displaces a coasting car sideways;
  - the wedge sweep with a gust applied at each wedge site stays escapable (Review Focus 1);
  - the 45 s demo on a pinned windy level makes at least one delivery in most of 10 runs (Review Focus 2; report the rate);
  - with no wind, no tumbleweed spawns and the bag is unchanged;
  - the windsock fits the HUD (`— hud layout —`);
  - the validator refuses a `strength` outside 0-1.
- [ ] **Step 2:** run them and see them FAIL.
- [ ] **Step 3:** implement. Render a windy frame with `tools/render/frame.mjs 5 20` and look at it: the windsock, dust, a tumbleweed.

    - the picture fills the height;
    - in the Delivery Shift, the stick is vertically centred in the left margin, and TOSS, GAS and REV are vertically centred in the right margin;

  - a fill at 12% alpha;
  - pressed: 35% fill and 85% outline.

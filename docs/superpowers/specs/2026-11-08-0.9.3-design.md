# 0.9.3: design

Everything here is the owner's ruling from 2026-11-07 or 2026-11-08 (recorded in `ROADMAP.md`'s
0.9.x list), unless marked *Claude's call*. This gathers them in one place.

## 1. Small tuning
- **Rain bites more** (Q2 B): rain grip goes from 0.84 to **0.75** (`WEATHER_GRIP.summer.rainy`).
- **The player's car stays red** (Q4 C): remove the red (#d94f4f) from the traffic colours
  (`CAR_COLORS`), so no traffic car is mistaken for the player.

## 2. One face per address (Q8 B)
- Each delivery address keeps **one customer face for the whole run**, picked from that
  decade's cast.
- *Claude's call:* the face is chosen the first time the address orders in a run, from the
  faces that have arrived by that level's year. It is the same face every later time that
  address orders, in this decade.
  - A new decade has a new map and new addresses, so they get new faces.
  - A new run (including CONTINUE) starts over.
- The Kitchen Shift's walk-in customers are not addresses and stay random.

## 3. Eyes on the 113 new faces
- The 104 new customers and the 9 cast have eyes cleared, so they do not blink.
- The owner asked Claude to set them. They are set **by hand** (looking at each face, not by the
  old detector) and written with `_eyesByHand: true`, so a re-bake keeps them.
- A face with sunglasses or goggles gets none, as `c90club` has.
- **The owner sees a proof first:** every face open and mid-blink, through the game's own
  drawing, before it ships.

## 4. Snow tracked in (E2)
- On **winter kitchen levels** (all December), the dining room's door mat shows tracked-in snow,
  and wet footprints lead along the line.
- This is decoration only: baked into the kitchen background once per level, never on summer
  levels.

## 5. A much simpler level card
- **Content:** "LEVEL #" and the date (month and year, e.g. DECEMBER 1972) on black.
- **Look:** concepts D and B together (`reference/level-card/2026-11-08-concepts.png`):
  - a thin three-colour stripe in the decade's colours;
  - the date typing on beneath it, in the decade colour;
  - a typewriter tick per letter.
- **Timing:** it holds about 2 seconds after the date finishes, then the shift starts by itself.
  Any key or tap skips ahead. There is no prompt text.
- **No menu at the start of a kitchen level.** The **pause screen keeps the menu**, at the cost
  of clock time (owner, 2026-11-08).
- The other cards (clear, game over, continue) are unchanged.

## 6. Weather set per level
- Each level in `content/levels.json` carries `weather`:
  - a summer level: `sunny` or `rainy`;
  - a winter level: `snowy` or `sunny`;
  - or `random`.
- A `windy` flag (true/false) stacks on top.
- **Random** rolls as today. Its windy roll is 30% in summer and 25% in winter (*Claude's
  call*).
- **The build refuses** snow in summer, rain in winter, and an unknown value.
- **Kitchen levels:** weather is decoration only (snow on the mat, rain on the dining window).
  Wind never affects the kitchen.
- **Service Mode's Levels tab** edits weather and windy.
- **Starting plan** (*Claude's*, approved by the owner):
  - Delivery 1 sunny; 3 snowy; 5 sunny + windy.
  - Delivery 7 rainy; 9 snowy + windy; 11 random.
  - Delivery 13 rainy + windy; 15 snowy + windy; 17 random.
  - Delivery 19 rainy + windy.
  - Kitchen: winter levels snowy; summer levels sunny, except 10 and 16 rainy.
- `?weather=` and the Test tab's override still win over the level's setting.

## 7. Windy (Delivery Shift only)
Three challenges:
1. **Crosswind on the toss.** The bag drifts downwind in flight, so the player aims upwind.
   - A **windsock** in the HUD shows direction and strength.
   - It fits beside the existing HUD panels; check with the `— hud layout —` section.
2. **Gusts shove the car** sideways for about a second.
   - Each gust is telegraphed about 1 second ahead: dust streaks across the screen and a GUST
     sound.
   - Gusts come every 8-14 seconds (*Claude's call*).
3. **Tumbleweeds** roll across the streets with the wind.
   - Hitting one bursts it into twigs and wobbles the car, with no fine.
   - They are drawn, never solid. The player can always drive through one.

Common rules:
- **Wind strength rises with difficulty** (*Claude's call*, values in `content/difficulty.json`):
  - Easy is gentle, Supreme strong.
  - A wind's direction is set per play; it is not part of `levels.json`.
- Swaying trees and dust streaks are the telegraph, not extra hazards.
- **The demo** drives windy levels too. It aims for the porch and accepts the drift.
- **Never breaks the guards:** a gust never pushes the car into a wedge (`unwedge` still runs)
  and never moves it through solid geometry (the `carBlocked` test applies).

## 8. Touch controls on the Pixel 10 (concept C, transparent)
`reference/touch-concepts/`; page https://claude.ai/artifact/1esoAeXXrpk3JgVCKbNwUJ.

- **Mode C in landscape:**
  - The game picture fills the full height.
  - **Delivery Shift:** ghost controls in the side margins:
    - the **stick vertically centred** on the left;
    - **TOSS, GAS and REV vertically centred** on the right;
    - outlines with a faint fill that brighten under the thumb.
  - **Kitchen Shift: no on-screen controls.** The player taps directly, as today; only pause
    remains.
- **Sizes:** stick about 18 mm, GAS about 15 mm. They are computed from the real device size, not
  fixed pixels. Keep 2-3 mm off the screen edge, away from Android's back swipe.
- **The minimap** (E3) moves to a free corner in Mode C, so the stick never covers it.
- **Near-native:**
  - full screen on the first tap, then `screen.orientation.lock('landscape')`;
  - `viewport-fit=cover` with safe-area insets for the camera cutout;
  - no pull-to-refresh or double-tap zoom;
  - a wake lock while playing.
  - **Netlify install:** a small `manifest.webmanifest` (`display: fullscreen`,
    `orientation: landscape`, icons) beside the deployed page. It is deploy-only: the
    single-file game still works opened as a file.
- **Verify on the owner's phone** over ADB (Chrome remote debugging, set up on the owner's PC)
  when this task is built: the CSS viewport, the DPR and the cutout.

## Not in scope
Regulars across decades; the rival as a car; the wind affecting the kitchen.

# Playtest notes, 2026-11-07

This was an hour of the game playing itself in Chrome (`taco-shop.html` over
`node serve.mjs`) while the owner watched. The game's own autoplayers had the
controls: `Demo` drove the delivery shifts and `Kitchen.botStep` cooked. A script
injected into the page started each level, moved past LEVEL CLEAR and handled the
game over. Claude took these notes. **No game code was changed during the playtest;**
every fix below is a suggestion waiting for the owner.

**What this can and can't tell us.** The autoplayers play competently, not like a
person: they don't get lost, panic or misread a bin. These notes catch bugs, pacing,
visuals and balance at a steady skill level. They don't capture feel.

## Bugs

### "TICKET 15ALL BAGGED": text running together from ticket 10 on

The BUILD PROGRESS readout draws `'TICKET ' + n` at x=6, and the next label at a
**fixed** x=60 (`src/74_kitchen.js`, `drawPrep`). The next label is "ALL BAGGED", or
"ITEM n OF m" plus its star. "TICKET 9" ends at x=53 and "TICKET 15" at x=59, so with
the outline the two strings butt together. Seen in L2 as **"TICKET 15ALL BAGGED"** and
in L8 as **"TICKET 11ITEM 2 OF 2"**. Every kitchen level reaches the teens, since a
level is 15 completions. Ticket 100 would overlap outright.

*Fix:* place the second label at `6 + textW('TICKET ' + t.n, 1) + 6`, with the star
after it as now. `— hud layout —` in the tests could assert the gap at ticket 99.

### At the shop, the nav says "ARRIVING / TOSS THE BAG"

L11: the bag was empty and the car was pulling up to the shop to restock. The panel read
**"ARRIVING / TOSS THE BAG"**. At the shop you load; you don't toss. `Hud`
(`src/70_hud.js:197`) prints TOSS THE BAG whenever `Nav.arriving`, whatever the goal is,
so it happens on every restock run. *Fix:* when the goal is the shop (`G.needPickup`, or
no order with an empty bag), say "PULL IN TO LOAD" or "RESTOCK".

### The off-screen distance label lands on the order card

When the target is off the top of the screen, the amber edge arrow clamps to the top of
its band. Its distance label ("39M", "30M") is drawn **17 px above the arrow**, which
puts it on the order card's tip bar. Seen in L1 and L5. In `Hud.edgeArrow`
(`src/70_hud.js:204`) the band's top is `VH/2 - 52` = y56, so the label lands around
y39, inside the card. *Fix:* draw the label **below** the arrow when it is in the top
half (`ey + 12`). Moving the band's top edge down instead would also work, but costs
precision.

### With an empty bag, the card says NO ACTIVE ORDER all the way back to the shop

Once the last taco is delivered there's no live order, so the card reads NO ACTIVE ORDER
for the whole drive back. Meanwhile the nav says "TURN LEFT IN 10 M" toward the shop.
"OUT OF TACOS - RESTOCK" only shows while an order is live. A one-off "OUT OF TACOS -
BACK TO SHOP" banner fires at that moment (`80_game.js:455`), but the card never says
where you're going. (Between ordinary orders the gap is about 1.5 s, which is fine.)
*Fix:* show a restock line on the card when `!order && bag === 0`.

## The main finding: RECALCULATING about a fifth of the time, mostly for no reason

The TACO-NAV panel kept reading "RECALCULATING. PLEASE WAIT" with the car on the road
and heading the right way. And while `glitch > 0.25`, `Nav.drawRoute` **hides the
chevrons on the road** too.

The cause is in `Nav.recompute` (`src/60_nav.js:107-110`). It re-solves the route every
0.4 s and sets `glitch = 0.45` whenever the route's key changes, if the old route had
more than one node. But the key changes in normal driving too.

Measured with `Nav.recompute` instrumented:
- **L1 (246 game-s):** 99 route-change flashes at 0.45 s each, plus 12 new orders at
  0.85 s each. That's about **55 s of RECALCULATING, or 22% of the shift.** 47 of the
  99 were only **the passed junction dropping off the front** of the route.
- **A finer split over another 121 flashes:**
  - 45 were again a passed junction.
  - 0 kept the next turn and changed only later in the route, so it isn't equal-cost
    flips further down.
  - 76 changed the route's **first node**. That's most likely the car **mid-turn**:
    `aheadNode` picks the junction ahead from the velocity, and as the car swings round a
    corner the velocity sweeps through both axes, so the "next junction" jumps. Real
    missed-turn re-routes are mixed in with these, but can't be told apart without
    logging positions.

*Suggested fix:* glitch only when the new route isn't a continuation of the old one.
Compare the new key against the old key with the passed head node dropped. Ignore
head-node changes while the car is inside a junction or turning. `— guidance —` in the
tests is the place to guard it. Note the 2026-08 `aheadNode` fix in CLAUDE.md; this
must not undo it.

## For the owner to decide

### Cop chases: CORRECTED, they were happening all along

**This note was wrong as first written.** It said no chase in 13 levels, but I had only
sampled `G.cop` at each check-in, every 2.5 minutes, and a chase lasts at most 26 s.
The **"tickets" column in the table above is the cop catching the car**
(`stats.tickets++` happens only in the pursuit, `80_game.js:962`). So L1–L13 had
**1–7 chases ending in a ticket on every delivery level**, plus any the autoplayer
escaped.

Measured headless afterwards, with `spawnCop` counted directly: **34 chases in 50
minutes of autoplayed driving** (L1, 5, 7, 11, 13; 2–5 per 5-minute run), with heat
reaching 100 in every run.

The owner asked for chases to be easier to trigger on the strength of the wrong
version of this note. That request has gone back to the owner with the corrected
numbers before anything changed.

### The kitchen is easy for the bot

Every kitchen level, Easy and Medium, cleared in **110–153 game-seconds** with 5–6
minutes still on the clock, and **zero walkouts** in six levels. The bot is faster than
a person, since it reads the hidden recipe and still slips on purpose, so a human will
take longer. The gap between the kitchen and the delivery shifts (246–548 s) is still
large. Worth watching in a human playtest.

### Hard is a real step up

L13 has no nav panel, only the dot and beacon, and it ended the run at 12/15. That
matches the headless sweep (about 1 in 2). A cliff at the first Hard level may be
intended. If not, the first Hard level could keep the panel.

### Winter is very pale

Snow on the ground and roofs, under the era grade, makes the frame low-contrast and
cream (L3, L9). Snow-covered parked cars read as faded or half-transparent. Up close it
reads correctly; at play size it's washed out. The player's car and the road lines still
pop, so gameplay is fine.

### Continuing resets the run's money

After the game over, CONTINUE? restarted L13 with the run total at $0. The board had
already recorded the $3,749.56, so nothing was lost, but confirm it's the intended rule.

## How the harness ran (for next time)

- **Chrome throttles an automated window.** `requestAnimationFrame` dropped to about
  1.5 fps, and timers to about 1 Hz, whenever no tool call was active. The game ran in
  slow motion. Not a game bug: a focused window runs at 60.
- **What worked:** a **Web Worker** posting ticks, ping-ponged so they can't queue up,
  driving `TacoShop.step()` paced to real time. 25 renders a second kept the page
  responsive. Steady 60 game-fps for the whole hour.
- **Two dead ends:**
  - Rendering every 16 ms (about 17 ms per render) froze the page out of the automation.
  - Ending the shift inside the `demo` state sends the game back to the attract screens,
    not GAME OVER. Check time-up before the demo update.
- **A first attempt was abandoned** because of those problems. Its L1 got 10/15. The
  clean restart, and the headless sweep (4/4 and 3/3), both clear L1.
- **The autoplayer types initials** on a placing game over, so back up and restore
  `localStorage` (`tacoshop.scores.v1`) around a run.

## Follow-up (same day): what was done

The owner asked for the bugs fixed, chases made easier, and rain added.
- **Fixed:**
  - **The ticket line** now places its label after the number. It reads "ITEM 1/3" so the
    line fits for any ticket under 1000.
  - **At the shop the nav says "STOP TO LOAD"**, not "TOSS THE BAG".
  - **The edge arrow's distance label** goes on the screen-centre side of the arrow.
  - **An empty bag with no order** reads "OUT OF TACOS - RESTOCK / HEAD BACK TO THE
    SHOP".
  - **RECALCULATING** now flashes only for a route that isn't the old one further along
    (`Nav.continues`), and not for flipping back to a route held a moment ago
    (`Nav.recent`). Measured on the autoplayer: **28.8% → 9.0%** of a shift in L1, and
    28.4% → 9.3% in L7. Most of what's left is the deliberate 0.85 s flash on each new
    order. A side effect: the autoplayer's deliveries in 240 s rose from 6 to 12, since
    the false flashes were also hiding the chevrons it reads.
- **Cop chases left as they are.** Once the corrected numbers were in (about one chase
  every 1.5 minutes for a reckless driver), the owner chose no change.
- **Weather added.** Rainy summers and sunny winters, rolled per play on Delivery levels.
  See `levels.md` and the GDD's §K.
- **The minor polish, done afterwards:**
  - **CONTINUE** now counts down to the next actual turn.
  - **The LOADING box** is pinned above the dock's bay.
  - **NEW ORDER** at the dock floats below the car.
- **Rain sound** added (`Audio5.rainOn/rainOff`), gated like the engine.

**What it says:**

- **Winter is now a coin flip between hard and easy.** Snow is the shipped winter
  difficulty. A sunny winter clears almost as often as summer, so half of all winter
  plays are now easier than they used to be, and a snowy Hard winter (L15) is the
  hardest thing in the game.
- *For the owner:* that variance may be the fun of it, or the snow chance in winter
  (`WEATHER_CHANCE`, currently 0.5 for both seasons) could be raised to keep winter
  mostly snowy. Nothing was changed.

# Kitchen Shift surroundings mockups

The three PNGs show actual 384×216 Kitchen Shift renders with three tickets on the rail, an active build, and the game's era grading and CRT pass. The bins, steam table, dining room, prep board, controls, and readouts come from the game. The pantry wall and counter are the design layer.

| Era | Visual direction | Shelf stock |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ’70s · July 1975 | Cool enamel tile, galvanized steel shelves, brushed-steel counter lip | Cans, jars, and an enamel jug above. Flour sacks, shell cartons, nested pans, and paper goods below. The mix gives the commercial pantry an everyday working rhythm. |
| ’80s · July 1984 | Warm lower-wall paneling, cool upper tiles, wood shelf trim, dark laminate counter | Red and cobalt cans, cartons, a squared jug, pans, and sleeves. Bright packaging separates from the paneling at game size. |
| ’90s · July 1993 | Cool blue laminate, periwinkle shelf trim, molded organizers, plastic bumper, and one taco promo poster | Totes, squeeze bottles, striped cartons, sleeves, and pans. The poster peeks out beneath the right-hand ticket during a busy shift. |

The calendar and jade-and-gold shop sign sit low on the wall. The brighter shelf shapes cluster between them. The counter is quiet under NOW BUILDING. Each image has a native-size version and a 3× nearest-neighbor review version. The uncropped image-generated pantry plates and prompts are in `source-plates/`.

# Taco Shop prep-board readability patch

Overlay the PNGs in `states/` onto the full set's `states/` directory. The 21 replacements retain their original filenames. The included manifest is byte-identical to the full set's manifest.

- House chili is a wider maroon pour with visible kidney beans and wet highlights.
- Refried beans over beef are lighter and pinker, with crumbles still visible around the spread.
- Mild sauce is wider over cheddar in Chili Burrito and Sour Cream Burrito.
- Later frames inherit the revised layers wherever they remain visible. The approved finish frames and chicken art are carried forward from the full set.

The comparison sheets show the frames immediately before and after each targeted ingredient pick, both original and revised, after 16-color reduction. `contrast-check.json` records the same RGB-distance threshold on each version. The two new ingredient cutouts were generated from well 16 and well 04 as style references. The source sheets and prompts are included.

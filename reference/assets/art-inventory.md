## Notes

### Candidate roles (unused)
- Officer: POLICE SERGEANT (70s Set 4), 80s COP (80s Set 3 and 5), 90s TV COP (90s Set 3), COP (ChatGPT 12_40_58, different style), MEN IN BLACK. Police Sergeant and 80s Cop are the strongest era fits.
- Rival delivery driver: RACE CAR DRIVER and TRUCK DRIVER (70s Sets 3/4), EASY RIDER, 80s BIKER, FEMALE TRUCKER, HELL'S ANGEL, AVIATOR, CYCLIST.
- Kitchen coworker: VIDEO STORE CLERK (80s Sets 1/5), VIDEO CLERK (90s Set 4), CHEF, TECH SUPPORT GUY, SKATEBOARDER, GRUNGE KID; the mockups also show an existing crew.

### Period-wrong for 1972-1999
- UPTIGHT KAREN (Misc 1) and NECK BEARD DUDE (Misc 2): labels are 2010s memes.
- DOPEY BUDDY (90s Set 3): "drew" smiley shirt is a 2019 brand.
- GAMER GUY (Misc 1): modern headset gaming look (probable).
- Everything else is period-plausible. Brands used (Atari, Intel Inside, Windows 95, Nintendo 64, Spice Girls, Nirvana, Blockbuster, Vans, Kangol, Adidas, Planet Hollywood, Charlotte Hornets) predate 2000. CYBERPUNK HACKER (Matrix-style code) sits at the 1999 edge. Real brands and IP are a trademark question, not a period error.

### Owner-judgment items (stated neutrally)
- Labels that name race: BLACK KID, ASIAN MAN, BLACK WOMAN IN CHURCH HAT, WHITE RAPPER (Misc Set 3). Depictions are plain but the labels are demographic.
- Black characters are concentrated in music and street roles across the sheets: DISCO KING, SOUL TRAIN GUY, JAZZ MUSICIAN, PIMP, D.J., BREAKDANCER (baked as c80breaker), 90s HIP HOP KID, R&B SINGER x2, RAPPER, HIP HOP HEAD, GANGSTA RAPPER, MIAMI CLUB KID. PIMP and GANGSTA RAPPER are the most trope-heavy.
- Religious: HARE KRISHNA (robes, tilaka, beads), NUN, PRIEST. PSYCHIC (twice) uses a fortune-teller headscarf and crystal ball that can read as a gypsy stereotype. GUY WITH BERET is a French stereotype.
- Sexual or content-sensitive: PORN STAR label (70s Set 2), FOXY LADY, NAKED GUY (shirtless), SKINNY SMOKING DUDE (cigarette; smokerbust also shows one), STONER DUDE.
- Demeaning or caricature: PIMPLY TEENAGE NERD, CHUBBY KID, COMPUTER NERD and TECH NERD (buck teeth), DOPEY BUDDY, CLUELESS BLONDE, TEEN MOM, GOOFY KID, MASKED ROBBER, HELL'S ANGEL (real club name), EASY RIDER (1% patch).
- Baked faces with a possible flag: c80breaker (above) and c90raider (Tomb Raider likeness, IP). The other baked faces carry no flagged label.
